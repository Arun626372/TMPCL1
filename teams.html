Category Assignment Rule

Categories Finalize After Trials

Registration ke time player category A/B/C/D choose nahi karega. Official trials ke baad category grade assign hoga, phir shortlisted players selection review me jayenge. Teams TMPCL rules ke hisaab se final squad banayengi.

🛡

D Category — Only U19

D category ke liye Date of Birth / valid age proof verification mandatory rahega. Har team me 3 D-category U19 players honge.
