Category Assignment Rule

Categories & Team Selection After Trials

Registration ke time player category A/B/C/D nahi choose karega. Official trials ke baad category grade assign hoga, phir shortlisted players Team Selection me jayenge. Teams selection process ke through apna final squad banayengi.

🛡

D Category — Only U19

D category players ke liye Date of Birth / Aadhaar / Age Proof verification mandatory rahega. Har team me 3 D-category U19 players honge.
