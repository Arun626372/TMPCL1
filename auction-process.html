Auction Flow

Trial Se Team Allocation Tak

Registration karne se direct team selection guarantee nahi hoti. Player ko trial performance ke basis par auction pool ke liye shortlist kiya ja sakta hai.

01

Player Registration

Player official partner portal se registration complete karega aur slip download karega.

02

Role-wise Trial

Batting, bowling, fielding, fitness aur discipline ke basis par performance evaluate hogi.

03

Golden Ticket / Shortlist

Strong performers ko auction pool eligibility ke liye shortlist kiya ja sakta hai.

04

Auction Pool

Eligible shortlisted players ko TMPCL Player Selection Auction Pool mein include kiya jayega.

05

Team Bidding / Selection

Teams apni requirement ke hisaab se auction/selection process mein players pick karengi.

06

Final Team Allocation

Final squad, team placement aur participation TMPCL official rules aur committee decision ke according hoga.

Important

Selection Guarantee Nahi Hai

Registration, Golden Ticket ya auction pool shortlist final team selection guarantee nahi karta. Final allocation team requirements, auction/selection process, player availability, discipline, fitness and TMPCL committee decision ke basis par hoga.
