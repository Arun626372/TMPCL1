TMPCL Official Policy

Privacy Policy

Tennis Madhya Pradesh Cricket League player trial registration, payment and support details.

Information We Collect

TMPCL registration form may collect player name, mobile number, email, city, date of birth, playing role, player photo and Aadhaar/Age Proof document for trial registration and verification.

How We Use Data

Player data is used for registration processing, age/category verification, trial communication, payment confirmation, team selection/selection workflow and official TMPCL support.

Payment Data

Payment is processed through authorized payment gateway partners. TMPCL does not store card, UPI PIN or bank password details. Payment status, order ID, transaction ID and amount may be stored for registration confirmation.

Data Security

Uploaded documents and registration records are stored in Supabase/database storage. Access is intended only for authorized TMPCL Team members managing registrations.

User Rights

Players may contact TMPCL support for correction of registration details or data-related queries. Some records may be retained for audit, payment and event management purposes.
