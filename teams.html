Category Assignment Rule

Categories & Auction After Trials

Registration ke time player category A/B/C/D nahi choose karega. Official trials ke baad category grade assign hoga, phir shortlisted players Player Auction me jayenge. Teams auction ke through apna final squad banayengi.

🛡

D Category — Only U19

D category players ke liye Date of Birth / Aadhaar / Age Proof verification mandatory rahega. Har team me 3 D-category U19 players honge.
