50 Balls. One Big Stage.

TMPCL The Fifty tennis cricket ko ek naye professional level par le jaane wala format hai. Traditional 6-ball over ki jagah yahan 5-ball sets rahenge, jahan har innings mein 50 legal balls hongi. Har set mein pressure, every powerplay mein strategy aur har shot mein league-level performance ka test hoga.

Player Help Desk

Frequently Asked Questions

Players ke common questions ka clear answer — registration, slip, trial rules, Golden Ticket aur Auction Pool process.

Registration fee kitni hai?

Player trial registration fee ₹999 hai. Ye fee player trial registration, verification, digital slip aur event coordination ke liye hai.

Registration aur payment kahan hoga?

Registration, payment processing aur slip access TMPCL ke official scoring & registration partner CrickBro portal ke through hoga.

Slip kaise download hogi?

Registration complete hone ke baad player official partner portal se registration slip download kar sakta hai.

Trial mein kya lekar aana hai?

Registration slip, valid ID proof, sports dress aur sports shoes compulsory rahenge. Age/category proof bhi saath rakhna chahiye.

Golden Ticket ka matlab kya hai?

Golden Ticket final selection guarantee nahi hai. Ye strong performance shortlist marker hai jo player ko Auction Pool eligibility ke liye mark kar sakta hai.

Auction Pool kya hai?

Trial performance ke basis par shortlisted players TMPCL Player Selection Auction Pool mein include ho sakte hain. Final team allocation auction/selection process ke through hoga.

Selection guarantee hai kya?

Nahi. Registration, Golden Ticket ya shortlist final team selection guarantee nahi karta. Committee decision final rahega.

The Fifty format kya hai?

Ye TMPCL ka 50-ball tennis cricket format hai: 10 sets of 5 balls, 15-ball powerplay, max 10 balls per bowler aur tie ke liye 5-ball Super Set.
