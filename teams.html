Category Assignment Rule

Categories Final After Trials

Registration ke time player category A/B/C/D nahi choose karega. A, B, C category official trials ke performance, fitness, experience aur selection panel evaluation ke baad assign hogi. D category sirf U19 players ke liye reserved rahegi.

🛡

D Category — Only U19

D category players ke liye Date of Birth / Aadhaar / Age Proof verification mandatory rahega. Har team me 3 D-category U19 players honge.
