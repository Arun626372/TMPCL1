TMPCL Official Policy

Refund & Cancellation

Tennis Madhya Pradesh Cricket League player trial registration, payment and support details.

Cancellation

Once player registration and payment are submitted, cancellation requests may be reviewed by TMPCL Team as per event status and verification stage.

Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be considered only for duplicate payment, failed payment debited from bank, technical payment mismatch or cancellation by TMPCL. Incorrect details or non-attendance may not qualify for refund.

Refund Timeline

Approved refunds, if any, will be processed to the original payment method as per payment gateway/bank timelines.

No Selection Guarantee

Registration fee is for trial registration and processing. Final selection, category grade, player auction or team selection is not guaranteed.

How To Request

For refund/cancellation requests, contact TMPCL support with Registration ID, player name, mobile number, payment/order ID and reason for request.
