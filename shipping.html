TMPCL Official Policy

Shipping & Delivery

Tennis Madhya Pradesh Cricket League player trial registration, payment and support details.

No Physical Shipping

TMPCL is an event/trial registration service. We do not ship physical products for player registration.

Digital Delivery

Registration confirmation, payment status, trial updates, venue/date information and related communication are delivered digitally through website status, phone, WhatsApp, email or official TMPCL announcements.

Confirmation Time

After successful payment verification, registration status is updated as Paid/Confirmed. If status does not update, player should contact TMPCL support with Registration ID and payment details.

Trial Updates

Trial venue, reporting time, admit card/pass or further instructions will be shared through official communication channels.

Support

For delivery/status issues related to registration confirmation, contact TMPCL support using the Contact page details.
