TMPCL Official Policy

Terms & Conditions

Tennis Madhya Pradesh Cricket League player trial registration, payment and support details.

Service Nature

TMPCL provides player trial registration and league-related participation opportunity. Registration fee is for trial participation processing, verification, digital registration slip and event coordination. It does not guarantee final team selection.

Registration Fee

The player trial registration fee is ₹999 unless changed officially by TMPCL. Payment must be completed through the approved checkout/payment gateway. Payment confirmation is required before the registration slip is treated as valid.

Eligibility & Documents

Players must provide correct name, mobile number, date of birth, city, playing role, photo and valid Aadhaar/Age Proof. Incorrect or fake documents may lead to rejection.

Trials & Selection

TMPCL Selection Committee may verify documents, evaluate player performance, assign category grade and shortlist players for team selection/final squad process as per league rules.

User Responsibility

Players are responsible for entering correct details and attending trials as per official schedule. TMPCL may update dates, venue, rules or requirements when necessary.
