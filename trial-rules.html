Official Rules

Player Trial Rules

Registration CompulsorySirf official partner portal se registered players hi trial mein participate kar payenge.
Slip & ID ProofPlayer ko registration slip, valid ID proof aur age/category proof ground par laana hoga.
Reporting DisciplinePlayer ko reporting time se 30 minutes pehle ground par report karna hoga.
Dress CodeSports dress aur sports shoes compulsory rahenge. Tennis ball cricket ke hisaab se pads/helmet compulsory nahi hain.
Batting Challenge5-Ball Power Set diya jayega. 14+ runs ko power-hitter benchmark maana jayega.
Bowling Challenge10-Ball Target Test mein line, length, control, speed, variation aur wide/no-ball control evaluate hoga.
Fielding & FitnessCatching, ground fielding, throwing accuracy, agility aur fitness selection ka important part hoga.
No Selection GuaranteeRegistration selection, match participation ya award guarantee nahi karta.
Committee Decision FinalAuction pool eligibility aur final team allocation performance, team requirement, auction/selection process aur committee decision ke basis par hoga.
Discipline RuleFake document, misbehavior, fighting ya ground rules todne par disqualification ho sakta hai.
Player Selection Auction Pool

Trial performance ke basis par eligible/shortlisted players ko TMPCL Player Selection Auction Pool mein include kiya ja sakta hai. Final team allocation auction/selection process, team requirement aur committee rules ke according hoga.

Golden Ticket

Shortlist Benchmark

Golden Ticket final squad selection guarantee nahi hai. Ye player ko TMPCL Player Selection Auction Pool ke liye shortlist marker bana sakta hai.

  • Batsman: 5 balls mein 14+ runs
  • Bowler: 10 balls mein 8+ quality balls
  • All-rounder: batting + bowling combined impact
  • Fielder: outstanding catch/throw/agility
